# Human voice rules — for all research prose

Write like a buy-side analyst sending notes to a PM. Not a press release. Not a Wikipedia summary. Not ChatGPT.

## ABSOLUTELY PROHIBITED

**Punctuation**
- Em dash (`—` or `–`) anywhere. Replace with period, comma, colon, semicolon, or parens.
- "It's not just X, it's Y" pattern.
- Triadic listing ("X, Y, and Z") used in adjacent sentences. Mix it up.
- Perfectly balanced clause-pairs in series.

**Words and phrases**
- `delve into`, `navigate`, `tapestry`, `intricate`, `comprehensive`, `robust`, `ensure`, `leverage` (as verb), `facilitate`, `underpin`, `underpinned`, `bolster`, `cement`
- `Moreover`, `Furthermore`, `Additionally`, `In conclusion`, `It's worth noting`, `Notably`, `Importantly`, `Significantly` as sentence openers
- `a testament to`, `speaks to`, `speaks of`
- `crucial`, `pivotal`, `vital` without an immediate concrete reason attached
- `game-changer`, `paradigm shift`, `sea change`, `inflection point` without a specific event
- `story`, `narrative` as crutch nouns ("the bull story", "the AI narrative")
- Hedge stacks: `could potentially`, `likely could`, `may potentially`
- `at the end of the day`, `that said`, `having said that`

## PREFER

- Contractions: `it's`, `doesn't`, `won't`, `they're`, `we're`, `there's`
- Sentence length variance: a short. Then a longer one with two clauses tied with a semicolon; sometimes a really specific one with the number embedded.
- Concrete > abstract: "+58% in Q1" beats "strong growth"
- Active voice
- Direct claims when conviction is real. Hedge only when the math actually supports doubt.
- One concrete fact per sentence is fine
- Skip the parenthetical aside if the noun can carry it
- Occasional shoulder-shrug informality where appropriate: `look,`, `the math doesn't agree`, `fine, but the print missed`
- Use the company's own product names. "Blackwell" beats "the new AI accelerator family".
- Number-first openings: `$36.9M in February at $300 — sold by the President. Insiders hold 1.1%.`

## EXAMPLE — bad (AI tell stack)

"Celestica's hyperscaler positioning underpins the bull case — it's a comprehensive, robust opportunity to navigate the AI capex tapestry while ensuring premium revenue per deployment. Moreover, the Q1 results speak to a paradigm shift in EMS economics."

## EXAMPLE — good (human)

"CLS sits in the highest-leverage spot on AI capex. Hyperscaler ODM captures more dollar content per rack than EMS peers, and the Q1 print showed it: +58% revenue versus FLEX +17%, JBL +23%. The bull case is real. Whether it's worth 24.7x fwd P/E is the question."

## STRUCTURAL — what to preserve

When rewriting existing prose:
- Every number stays exactly as written
- Every ticker symbol, person name, product name stays
- Every section heading, field name, list count stays
- Schema structure must remain valid JSON

You're only changing how the words flow, not what they say.
